Editions and licensing

Two editions, plus vertical packages. The governance is in both editions.

Community under the BSL use grant is free, with every governance feature in it. Enterprise is for organisations above the use grant and for regulated deployments that need an SLA and someone to call. Vertical packages are configuration artifacts that install on either edition.

02 What is in each edition

Governance is not the upsell.

Row-level security, column masking, the append-only query audit log, ABAC hooks and SSO are in the Community edition. They are not paywalled, and no edition or tier check gates them (REQ-1137).

In the Community edition

Row-level security (README.md:124), column masking (README.md:125), the append-only query audit log, ABAC approval hook (README.md:130) and pluggable auth including Firebase and Keycloak OIDC (README.md:131). Governance ships with the product, not above it.

What Enterprise adds

SLA guarantees, dedicated support, advanced audit logging and compliance reporting (docs/marketing.md:22). Priced by contract (docs/marketing.md:15).

04 Two different things

The free licence file is not the production grant.

Two sentences, kept adjacent because conflating them is how a reader would be misled by accident:

REGISTRATION The free licence file is a registration record. It does not grant production rights; the BSL does.

The in-product licence is free, validates offline, is tied to a machine, and after a 30-day trial an unregistered deployment shows a non-fatal notice on every supported interface and never gates or degrades anything (REQ-1138 offline Ed25519 verification; REQ-1139 machine_id binding; REQ-1136 30-day trial expiry; REQ-1137 non-fatal notice, never gates or degrades). The BSL Additional Use Grant above is what decides whether you may run Provisa in production at all. One is free for everyone; the other is free only below the threshold.

Provisa collects no telemetry and does not phone home; the licence validates without network access, which is what makes an air-gapped deployment possible (REQ-1137; docs/deployment.md:12,:24).

05 Consequence

What this means for most of our readers.

Most organisations this site is written for are over 100 people or over USD 1,000,000, so most of our readers need a commercial licence to run Provisa in production — not to host it for others, not to unlock a feature, to run it. The licence we publish is a clear line rather than a hidden one.

PLAIN FORM Every governance feature is in the Community edition. What you buy is the right to run it in production, plus an SLA and someone to call.
