03

Spacing & rules

Zero corner radius everywhere except the role switch (pill) and dot-grid circles. Structure comes from rules, not boxes: 1px hairlines between rows, 1px ink above card groups, 3–4px brand-colored top rules on feature cards, 6px vermilion left edge on "PLAN OUT" callouts.

Spacing · cardSpacing scaleGaps used in the source — exact, not grid-snapped
Spacing · cardRules & accentsSquare corners; rules do the structural work
04

Brand

The wordmark is a CSS construction — three 6×18px bars (cobalt · forest · vermilion, 3px gap) with "Provisa" in Source Serif 4 at 26px. No logo file exists. Illustration is flat geometric "FIG." panels; the source has no photography.
