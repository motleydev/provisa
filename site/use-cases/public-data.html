Use Cases · Public and Private Data

Private data in public context. One governed query.

An organization's own data is most useful beside the public context it sits in: population, prices, employment, weather, filings. Provisa joins the two in one governed query, with the private side's rules intact. A retailer chooses store locations with sales beside Census and BLS data. A bank's credit-risk team wants exposures beside industry employment trends. A hospital network plans staffing with admissions and flu surveillance. A utility reports emissions with grid-mix data. An investment analyst wants filings beside the firm's internal ratings.

The government open-data source (Census, BLS and other federal series) registered once, joined to private tables through approved relationships. Store-level revenue stays limited to the finance role while the public figures are open to all. Borrower names are masked for every role but credit risk. Patient-level rows never leave the clinical role; the planning view exposes only daily counts. The audit log shows who queried which resources when.

01Retail site selection

A retailer choosing where to open its next ten stores wants its own sales per store beside each surrounding area's population growth, median income, and competitor density.

The store and sales tables are joined to Census and BLS series from the government open-data source on county and ZIP code. Analysts query it in SQL or ask the chat assistant in plain language. Store-level revenue stays limited to the finance role while the public figures are open to all.

02Credit risk and employment

A bank's credit-risk team wants each commercial borrower's exposure alongside its industry's employment trend and its county's unemployment rate.

Loan exposures from the core banking warehouse (Snowflake) are joined to federal employment and unemployment series on industry code and county. A materialized view refreshes when the public series publish new data. The risk dashboard reads it over GraphQL. Borrower names are masked for every role but credit risk.

03Hospital staffing forecast

A hospital network plans staffing for flu season using its own admissions history and the public flu-surveillance and weather data for each facility's area.

Admissions from the EHR are joined to public health surveillance and weather feeds by region and week. Patient-level rows never leave the clinical role; the planning view exposes only daily counts per facility, which the workforce team and a forecasting model read over Arrow Flight.

04Utility emissions disclosure

A utility's sustainability team must report emissions intensity per customer segment against regional grid-mix and climate data published by the government.

Metered usage from the utility's own systems joined to public grid-mix and emissions-factor series. The disclosure is a versioned view on the model's release branch, so the figures auditors check are reproducible against that exact model version. The audit log shows every query behind them.

05Investment research

An investment analyst wants a company's reported numbers alongside its peers' public filings and the macro series that drive its sector, plus the firm's internal ratings.

Public filings and macro series from the government open-data source are joined to the firm's internal ratings table, which only the research role can see. The analyst's agent queries both through MCP. The internal ratings never reach a role or surface that isn't entitled to them.
