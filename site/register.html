Register your Provisa install.

A licence is free — it exists only so we can gather basic usage information, because Provisa collects no telemetry and never phones home. Functionality is not affected or degraded whether or not you register.

Registering doesn't mean you owe anything: under the Business Source License 1.1, non-production use — including evaluation — is free forever, for any size organization. Production use is also free for any organization that, together with its affiliates, has fewer than 100 employees and contractors and under $1,000,000 in annual revenue, budget, or expenditures — most people registering here qualify. Provisa is self-hosted: your infrastructure, your cloud, or air-gapped. Questions about a commercial licence: license@provisa.dev.
