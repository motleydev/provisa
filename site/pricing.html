Provisa / Pricing
Pricing

Two editions, plus vertical packages. The governance is in both editions.

Governance is not the upgrade. Row-level security, column masking, the approval hook and the append-only access record are in the Community edition. Enterprise adds the things an SLA names.

Community is free under the use grant and self-hosted. Enterprise is self-hosted or private cloud, including air-gapped, and priced by contract. The vertical packages are config artifacts that layer on either edition.

02 Who each edition is for

The line between the two is a procurement line, not a capability line

COMMUNITY Any individual or organisation under the use grant. Evaluation is free for any size organisation at any time, under the non-production right. Production use is free under the thresholds in §4, with a 90-day grace period on crossing them.
ENTERPRISE Organisations above the use grant, and regulated enterprises that need SLA and support. The licence is the same shape; what changes is the commercial relationship and what someone will sign to carry it.
03 What Enterprise adds

Four additions, named

01

SLA guarantees

Response times, uptime where Provisa runs on infrastructure we can observe, and remedies written into the contract rather than offered in a readme.
02

Dedicated support

A named engineer on the other end of an escalation, with a route that bypasses the public issue queue.
03

Advanced audit logging

The append-only access record is in the Community edition. Enterprise adds the retention, export and management-reporting layer that a compliance programme asks for.
04

Compliance reporting

Pre-built reports that line up with the control narratives an auditor reads, over the ops tables that the Community edition already exposes.

05 The free licence file

A registration record, not a production right

The free licence file is a registration record. It does not grant production rights; the BSL does. What the file carries, and what the running software does with it, is published per requirement:

01

A one-time registration capture

The file is issued once per registration. The licence is free, and nothing is charged or blocked by the file itself. REQ-1138, REQ-1137.
02

Offline Ed25519 verification

Signature verification happens in process, with no call to a licence server. An air-gapped installation reads the file the same way a connected one does. REQ-1138.
03

Bound to this installation

The file is bound to a machine_id, so the status is reported on every supported interface — the ones with a safe out-of-band channel. REQ-1139.
04

Expired at 30 days is non-fatal

An expired registration record notices on the surfaces it can safely notice on and keeps running. Nothing is gated and nothing is degraded. REQ-1136, REQ-1137.
06 SaaS

Not currently offered

SAAS Provisa does not currently offer a managed service. Deployments today are self-hosted or on a private cloud you operate, including air-gapped. If zero operations is your requirement, this page is honest about where we are not — /compare names it as a case where Provisa is the wrong answer, and we would rather tell you.
