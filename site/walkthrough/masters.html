Provisa / Walk through it / Slide masters

The deck layer

Eleven masters, and a deck is a composition of them.

Every Provisa briefing slide is one of the eleven masters below, and every master is a composition of partials the website already ships. The slides, the walkthrough and the exported PNGs come from one source, site/design-system/walkthrough.css, so a slide cannot drift from the site. It is the site.

The copy on each plate describes the master it sits on, not the product itself. The three briefings that use these masters are at /walkthrough.

M4 · Two-column argument .pv-sl--columns

Two columns, split by a hairline.

The left column takes the argument. The measure is capped so a line never runs past comfortable reading length, which on a slide matters more than it does on a page: a reader has seconds, not minutes.

The right column takes the consequence. A vertical rule rather than a gap, because this system does its separating with hairlines and has no card to put the columns in.

M5 · Ruled list .pv-sl--ruled

Rows, numbered and ruled.

  1. 01A row opens with its index. Mono, in the deck's accent, so the eye can count without reading.
  2. 02The hairline is the only divider. No alternating fill, no card, no shadow.
  3. 03Six rows is the ceiling. A seventh belongs on a second slide.
