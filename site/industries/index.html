Industries

Four regulated verticals, one governed model.

The same four mechanisms do the regulated work in every one of them: column visibility, row filters, column masking and an audit record of every governed query — compiled into the plan at one place every surface goes through, and running as regional clusters on one shared model where the data lives where it is required to live.

The hub names those mechanisms once. The vertical pages show them doing the scenario a buyer in that industry brings — a procurement step, a reporting obligation, a cross-team isolation, a cross-border read.

01 The horizontal

What is the same across every vertical.

Each mechanism is one place in the model, written once, and the vertical page uses it against the data its reader cares about. Nothing on this list is per-industry.

01

Column visibility

A column names the roles that may see it, in visible_to. A role without the column does not get it withheld — the column is not in the schema its session resolves. If a table has no column the role may see, the table is dropped from the role's schema entirely. REQ-1131, REQ-1614, REQ-1778.
02

Column masking

A visible column may be masked for a role: the value comes back redacted, truncated or substituted rather than withheld. The mask is expressed in the governed IR and pushed down into the statement that reaches the source. REQ-971, REQ-1494.
03

Row filters

A table names a rls_rules entry per role: a predicate that is injected into every SELECT for that role after compilation and before execution, through subqueries, CTEs, joins and star expansion alike.
04

Audit of every governed query

Every governed query lands in the append-only audit log: the user, the role, the query hash, the tables touched, the surface it came over, the status, the duration and what was enforced on the statement. The log stores table ids and a query hash — not the data. REQ-596, REQ-613.
02 Where the data lives

Region-homed data, one shared model.

A table names its home region. That is the only place its whole-table copies are built. A reader in another region is served from that replica, in place, under the reader's own row and column rules — the model is one, the clusters are not.

THE SHAPE Each region runs its own engine, storage and cache on one shared model. Reads from other regions are served in place under the reader's rules. The model is shared and any node can reach any source; what is isolated is the engine, the replica storage, the cache and the per-region record. REQ-1921, REQ-1922.
03 The four

Four verticals, four pages.

Each card is a page. The scenario and the mechanism it rests on are named on the card so the reader can tell whether the page below it is the one they came for.

01

Cybersecurity

Deployment inventory joined to SBOMs, scanner outputs and the cloud inventory on an approved relationship, with a materialized view that lists noncompliant builds and open CVEs by application.

 02

Financial services

Positions and prices as views with history, read as of a past time and as recorded then — X-Provisa-As-Of and X-Provisa-As-Recorded — so a later correction does not change what the auditor saw.

 03

Healthcare

PHI columns absent from a researcher's schema, a consent predicate injected into every read, and audit trails for HIPAA reporting of who read a PII-tagged table under which role.

 04

Government

Multi-agency data isolation through domains: a reference from one agency's role into another agency's domain is refused before execution, and a table's region pins its copies to one cluster.
WHAT THIS PAGE DOES NOT CLAIM Where a compliance programme is named on this page or on the four vertical pages, it is named as a compliance hook — the mechanisms Provisa offers that an auditor working to that programme can use. Provisa holds no certification and no attestation, in any of them. The page's claim is about the mechanism it names, not about an accreditation it does not hold.
