Network, billing, customers. Different systems, different countries.

Network, billing and customer data that sit in different systems and different countries. A network operations centre wants to know, during an outage, which enterprise customers are affected, what their SLAs promise, and whether a credit is due. A group operating in several EU countries must keep subscriber call records in each country, yet the group marketing team wants churn by product across all of them.

01Scenario one — Outage impact

A network operations centre wants to know, during an outage, which enterprise customers are affected, what their SLAs promise, and whether a credit is due.

Live alarm events from the network monitoring feed (Kafka) joined to the inventory of cells and circuits, the customer contracts in Salesforce and billing in the warehouse. A view lists affected customers with their SLA terms. The NOC agent reads it over MCP, and a command opens a ServiceNow incident per affected enterprise customer.

Related: Enterprise SaaS and workflow, Real-time operations

02Scenario two — Cross-border churn

A group operating in several EU countries must keep subscriber call records in each country, yet the group marketing team wants churn by product across all of them.

One model with regions: each country's subscriber tables live in its own region. A materialized view builds churn by product in each region, and only those aggregates are visible to the group role, so no subscriber row leaves its country. The audit log shows the regulator who read what.
