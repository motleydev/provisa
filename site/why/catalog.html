Catalog

It feeds the catalog you already bought.

Provisa is not another place your metadata goes to die. The model that governs your queries publishes outward to the tools your organization already runs — and addresses every governed value it holds.

01 Where it publishes

Publishes to your catalog.

One registration, six destinations: OpenMetadata, DataHub, Collibra, Atlan, Apache Atlas, and OpenLineage. Tables, columns, tags, classifications, and lineage flow out as first-class catalog assets, from the admin console or from provisa metadata export on a cron.

The point is not that Provisa has a catalog. It is that the metadata is a byproduct of the thing that already has to be correct — the model that executes and governs your queries — rather than a parallel description maintained by hand and drifting from the moment it is written.

02 Lineage

Lineage that survives re-platforming.

Assets are stitched on business identity, not physical location. Move a table from Oracle to Postgres and its catalog entry, lineage, and history follow the business entity instead of shattering into a new asset with no past. A migration doesn't reset your catalog to zero.

03 Quality

Data quality, without a second engine.

Point Soda at a governed table and register the contract as a source. You get scan history, a quality scorecard, and lineage from each failing check back to the table it guards — with scans triggered by change rather than by a cron guess. Provisa ships no data-quality engine and doesn't ask you to retire yours.

04 Addressing

Every value has an address.

A governed value is addressable as provisa://<org>/<domain>/<table>/pk=<id>/<column> and resolves over REST or MCP — optionally as of a point in time. Exported documents, lineage nodes, audit records, and AI-agent citations carry links that resolve back to the live governed value, under the same policy as any other read.

Only semantic addresses dereference. Physical URIs are refused, so a citation can never leak a location — which matters most when the thing doing the citing is an agent whose output you cannot review line by line.
